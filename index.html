Our Story

Georgian warmth, on Elm Street.

GEAMOS is a Georgian café and bakery in the heart of Westfield, New Jersey. We bring the spirit of a Georgian family kitchen into a bright, welcoming space where neighbors linger over coffee and friends gather around a shared table.

Our bakery is built on traditional recipes and simple ingredients, shaped by hand and baked with patience. From khachapuri to honey cake, every dish is meant to feel like a little piece of home, whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth.
